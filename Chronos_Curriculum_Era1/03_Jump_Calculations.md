# 🧮 Lesson 03: Jump Calculations
**Topic:** Int, Float, Math (`+ - * /`) | **Era:** Navigation Deck

## 🎬 Phase 1: Mission Briefing (Slides 1-6)
1.  **Title Card:** "JUMP CALCULATIONS". `**[VISUAL: Holographic star map with complex trajectory lines]**`
2.  **The Hook:** Space travel requires precision. If the math is off by 0.1, we fly into a sun.
3.  **Concept:** **Integers** (Whole bricks) vs **Floats** (Liquid/Decimals).
4.  **Vocab:** **Operator** (The symbol that does the math).

## 🧠 Phase 2: The Intel (Slides 7-14)
7.  **Symbols:** `+` (Add), `-` (Sub), `*` (Star, not X!), `/` (Divide).
8.  **The Division Rule:** Division `/` ALWAYS creates a Float. `10 / 2` = `5.0`.
9.  **Math with Variables:** `fuel = tank - used`.
10. **String Math:** `"10" + "10"` = `"1010"` (Sticking stickers together).
11. **Int Math:** `10 + 10` = `20`.
12. **Crash:** Division by Zero. `**[VISUAL: Black hole explosion]**`
13. **Quiz:** What is `"10" * 2`? (Answer: `"1010"`).

## 💻 Phase 3: The Uplink (Slides 15-22)
*Scenario: Calculating Distance.*
15. **Setup:** `speed = 500`.
16. **Micro-Step 1:** `time = 2`.
17. **Micro-Step 2:** `distance = speed * time`.
18. **Micro-Step 3:** `print(distance)`.
19. **Output:** `1000`.
20. **Modify:** Change time to `2.5`.
21. **Result:** `1250.0` (Float!).
22. **Interactive:** Calculate how many seconds in 5 minutes.
