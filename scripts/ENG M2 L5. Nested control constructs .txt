Task 1. Program a textual bot that can keep up a conversation. The bot must reply to these questions:
Q: “What are you able to do?” A: “I can talk with you if you feel sad.”
Q: “Tell a joke.” A: “The first five days after the weekend are always the hardest!”

question = input('Ask me anything (off - exit):')
while question != 'off':
   if question == 'What are you able to do?':
       print('I can talk with you if you feel sad.')
   elif question == 'Tell a joke.':
       print('The first five days after the weekend are always the hardest!')
   else:
       print('Sorry, I don't understand you.')
   question = input('Ask me anything (off - exit):')

Hackathon rules
Brainstorm

A hackathon is a competition among developers. 
Before a hackathon, the organizer suggests a project idea and the form in which that idea should be implemented.
The participants then work on the idea for some time.
At the end, the results are presented and discussed by both the judges and the participants.
Hackathons can be conducted in teams or individually.
